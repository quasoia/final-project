YOUR WELLBEING 🌊

You Don't Have to Navigate Alone

Being a teenager can feel overwhelming sometimes. Taking care of your mental health starts with small, supportive steps.

💙

Talk to Someone

Share how you're feeling with someone you trust, such as a friend, parent, teacher, or school counselor.

🌱

Take Small Steps

Give yourself time to rest, do things you enjoy, and focus on one manageable step at a time.

☀️

Be Kind to Yourself

You do not have to be perfect. Your feelings matter, and asking for help is a sign of strength.

🐚

It's Okay to Ask for Help

If things feel difficult for a while, consider talking with a trusted adult, school counselor, or mental health professional. You deserve support.
